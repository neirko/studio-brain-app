Privacy policy

studio-brain requests read-only access to YouTube data (scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly) for channels owned by its single user.

  • Data read: channel analytics (views, watch time, average view percentage, traffic sources) of the owner's own channels.
  • Storage: results are saved only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server or third party.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

studio-brain's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
